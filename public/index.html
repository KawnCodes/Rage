Manual connect

Swap two codes with your friend: AirDrop, Messages, anything. Codes contain your network addresses, so only share them with people you trust.

📱 Two iPhones, no internet?
  1. Both phones need RAGE saved for offline play (open it once while online, then Share → Add to Home Screen).
  2. Phone A: Settings → Personal Hotspot → turn on. Phone B: join Phone A's Wi-Fi. (Or put both on the same Wi-Fi.)
  3. Phone A taps I'm hosting, then Share → AirDrop to Phone B.
  4. Phone B taps I'm joining, pastes the code, taps Make reply code, then Share → AirDrop back.
  5. Phone A pastes the reply and taps Connect.

AirDrop uses Bluetooth to find the other phone, so this works with no internet at all. The game itself then talks over the hotspot's Wi-Fi.

Touch controls show up automatically on phones and tablets. In local 2 player each player gets their own set: P1 on the left, P2 on the right.

Hold jump to jump higher. R restarts (counts as a death). Esc pauses. H goes home. M mutes. In menus, use arrow keys / D-pad and Enter / A.

Controllers & Bluetooth

Pair any Bluetooth (or USB) gamepad with your device, then press a button on it. Controller 1 → P1, controller 2 → P2. With one controller in local 2P, it plays P2 and the keyboard plays P1.

Browsers can't link two devices to each other directly over Bluetooth (Web Bluetooth only talks to accessories), so two-device play uses the online lobby. It also works on a local Wi-Fi / hotspot.
