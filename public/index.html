Controls

Solo / OnlineLocal P1Local P2
MoveA D / ← →A D← →
JumpSpace W ↑ ZW / Space↑ / Num0

Touch controls show up automatically on phones and tablets. In local 2 player each player gets their own set: P1 on the left, P2 on the right.

Hold jump to jump higher. R restarts (counts as a death). Esc pauses. H goes home. M mutes. In menus, use arrow keys / D-pad and Enter / A.

Controllers & Bluetooth

Pair any Bluetooth (or USB) gamepad with your device, then press a button on it. Controller 1 → P1, controller 2 → P2. With one controller in local 2P, it plays P2 and the keyboard plays P1.

Browsers can't link two devices to each other directly over Bluetooth (Web Bluetooth only talks to accessories), so two-device play uses the online lobby. It also works on a local Wi-Fi / hotspot.
